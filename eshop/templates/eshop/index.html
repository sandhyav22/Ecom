FAQ

1. How do I place an order on the website?

⌄

You can browse the categories, select your products, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Choose your
delivery address and complete payment to place the order.

2. Do I need an account to place an order?

⌃

Yes, you need to create an account or log in. This helps us savw\e our address, track our orders, and
provide a better shopping experience.

3. How do I register as a seller?

⌃

Click on "Become a Seller", enter your mobile number, business details, GST number(if applicable), pickup address,
and bank details to complete our registration.

4. Are all products available for instant delivery?

⌃

Most products are available for fast delivery. Products availability may vary based on your location and
inventory.

5. How do I reorder items I previouly purchased?

⌃

You can reorder quickly by clicking "Reorder" in your order history. All previoussly bought items will be
added to the cart.
